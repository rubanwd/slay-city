## Project overview

# SLAY CITY — Project Description

SLAY CITY is a gamified English-learning Progressive Web App for children aged 7–14. The app turns daily English practice into an adventure inside a virtual city. Instead of completing traditional lessons, students help Slay, a friendly snake mascot, restore the city by completing short daily English missions.

Each completed mission unlocks a new location on the city map, introduces new vocabulary, rewards the student with coins and XP, and visually moves Slay forward. The main goal of the product is to make English learning feel like exploration, progress, and play rather than a regular study task.
